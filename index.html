About Rewel Global Solution

Rewel Global Solution is a premier consulting firm based in Kenya. We specialize in providing expert advice, document verification, and step-by-step assistance for individuals and corporations dealing with passports, immigration visas, work permits, and vital registration documents.

We understand that governmental and embassy guidelines can be intricate and constantly shifting. Our mission is to bridge the gap by offering expert knowledge, meticulous checking, and administrative support to ensure your application materials are flawless prior to submission.
